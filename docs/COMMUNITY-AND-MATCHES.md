# Cosmic Scrap community and sharing

All social posts are opt-in. Buttons open X's post composer with text prefilled; nothing is posted automatically.

## Prepared post types

- **New record:** "NEW COSMIC SCRAP RECORD: [score] points · level [level] · survived [seconds]s. I did it — can you beat my run? Play free: [game URL]"
- **Record challenge:** "My Cosmic Scrap record is [score] points. Your turn — can you beat it? Play free: [game URL]"
- **Friend invite:** includes the room URL, short invite code, level bracket and friendly challenge prompt.
- **Welcome/community:** invites a pilot to try a run and share their own best score.

## Healthy retention loop

1. Let new players start immediately without an account.
2. Offer an account only when they want cloud identity, online records, friends or matchmaking.
3. Show a real personal best and a clear, optional share action.
4. Give players a level-banded opponent and a result they can understand.
5. Encourage friendly rivalry; avoid fake live counts, false urgency, streak punishment or claims that friends are waiting when they are not.

## Current limits

Matches are asynchronous score challenges, not real-time co-op. Client-reported results are not fully cheat-proof. Do not attach valuable prizes to them until a trusted validation path exists.


## Safety and moderation

See [COMMUNITY-SAFETY.md](./COMMUNITY-SAFETY.md) for the chat rules, report/block flow, server-side message filtering and rate limits, suspension design, moderator setup, and Atatürk portrait attribution. The current release includes player reporting and blocking, but no in-game moderator dashboard or appeal flow yet.
