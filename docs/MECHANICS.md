# Mechanics Reference

## Combat
The player automatically targets the nearest living enemy. Player projectiles travel in a straight line and can pierce enemies. Critical hits deal double damage. Enemy contact or violet ranged shots damage the player, followed by a brief invulnerability window.

## Scrap and levels
Defeated enemies drop scrap orbs. Orbs gravitate toward the player inside pickup range. Collected value is multiplied by the active combo multiplier. XP requirements use a steeper level curve (18 × level^1.45, rounded up); level-up pauses combat until an upgrade is selected.

## Combo
Kills extend a four-second timer. Multiplier rises by 0.035 per kill, capped at x2.8. Maximum combo is recorded in run stats.

## Difficulty
Spawn intervals shrink toward a floor. Health, movement speed and incoming damage scale with elapsed time. Enemy types: normal, fast, tank, swarm and shooter. Shooters appear after the opening phase, try to keep distance, and fire visible violet projectiles; their share rises later in a run. The red boundary closes after the opening minute; remaining outside it can cause damage.

## Cosmic Storm
Storm cycles begin after four minutes and last 18 seconds in each 92-second cycle. Storm kills award bonus score.

## Meta and idle
Runs grant Scrap Core based on collected scrap. Local storage records best score and permanent ranks. Returning later grants a capped offline scrap bonus of up to eight hours. Progress is local-device only, not cloud-synced.

## Input
WASD / arrow keys move. Hold pointer or touch on the arena to move toward it. P or Escape pauses/resumes. Space or Enter selects the first upgrade while the level-up choice is open.
