# Mechanics Reference

## Combat
The player automatically targets the nearest living enemy. Player projectiles travel in a straight line and can pierce enemies. Critical hits deal double damage. Enemy contact or violet ranged shots damage the player, followed by a brief invulnerability window.

## Scrap and levels
Defeated enemies drop scrap orbs. Orbs gravitate toward the player inside pickup range. Collected value is multiplied by the active combo multiplier. XP requirements use a fast first level, then rise as ceil(12 × level^1.65); level-up pauses combat until an upgrade is selected.

## Combo
Kills extend a four-second timer. Multiplier rises by 0.035 per kill, capped at x2.8. Maximum combo is recorded in run stats.

## Difficulty
Spawn intervals shrink toward a floor. Health, movement speed and incoming damage scale with elapsed time. Enemy types include normal, fast, swarm, tank, heavy tank, shooter, sniper, exploder and spawner. Shooters maintain mid-range lanes and fire; snipers stay much farther away and launch slower, high-damage shots; exploders rush the player and damage nearby enemies when defeated; spawners stay behind the frontline and release three swarm units on death. Ranged and special-unit pressure grows as the run progresses. The red boundary closes after the opening minute; remaining outside it can cause damage.

## Cosmic Storm
Storm cycles begin after three minutes. Their interval gradually falls from 92 seconds toward 58 seconds, while active duration grows from 18 seconds toward 30 seconds. Storm kills award bonus score.

## Meta and idle
Runs grant Scrap Core based on collected scrap. Local storage records best score and permanent ranks. Returning later grants a capped offline scrap bonus of up to eight hours. Progress is local-device only, not cloud-synced.

## Input
WASD / arrow keys move. Hold pointer or touch on the arena to move toward it. P or Escape pauses/resumes. Space or Enter selects the first upgrade while the level-up choice is open.
