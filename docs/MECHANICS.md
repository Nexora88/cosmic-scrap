# Mechanics Reference

## Combat
The player automatically targets the nearest living enemy. Projectiles travel in a straight line and can pierce enemies. Critical hits deal double damage. Enemy contact damages the player, followed by a brief invulnerability window.

## Scrap and levels
Defeated enemies drop blue scrap orbs. Orbs gravitate toward the player inside pickup range. Collected value is multiplied by the active combo multiplier. XP thresholds grow with level; a level-up pauses combat until an upgrade is selected.

## Combo
Kills extend a four-second timer. Multiplier rises by 0.035 per kill, capped at x2.8. Maximum combo is recorded in run stats.

## Difficulty
Spawn interval falls toward a floor. Enemy health and speed scale with elapsed time. Types: normal, fast, tank, and swarm. A red boundary begins closing after the first minute; remaining outside it can cause damage.

## Cosmic Storm
Storm cycles begin after six minutes and last 18 seconds in each 115-second cycle. Storm kills award bonus score.

## Meta and idle
Runs grant Scrap Core based on collected scrap. Local storage records best score and permanent ranks. Returning later grants a capped offline scrap bonus of up to eight hours. Progress is local-device only, not cloud-synced.

## Input
WASD / arrow keys move. Hold pointer or touch on the arena to move toward it. P or Escape pauses/resumes. Space or Enter selects the first upgrade while the level-up choice is open.
