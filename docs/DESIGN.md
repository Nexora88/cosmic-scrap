# Design Vision

## Pillars
1. **Minimal clarity:** pure black arena and readable neon geometry.
2. **Constant agency:** the player moves; targeting and firing are automatic.
3. **Frequent decisions:** each level offers three mutually exclusive upgrades.
4. **Run-to-run growth:** Scrap Core progression provides small permanent bonuses.
5. **Readable onboarding:** the first minute teaches the loop through play.

## Visual language
- Player: white square with restrained bloom.
- Enemies: red geometric silhouettes; size and speed signal archetype.
- Scrap and projectiles: cyan and ice-blue accents.
- Cosmic Storm: subtle red tint and high-contrast warning.
- UI: compact uppercase typography, thin borders, subtle procedural starfield, distinct warning geometry for major encounters.

## Experience curve
- 0–60s: low pressure and readable enemy spacing.
- 1–3m: upgrade-driven power curve.
- 3–6m: threat density tests movement.
- 6m+: storm cycles and increasing boundary pressure.

## Product boundaries
The browser game is free and ad-free. Trading, Steam integration, and cosmetic drops are future possibilities only; no real-money economy is implemented.
