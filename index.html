SCIENCE · CURIOSITY · THE FUTURE

Atatürk's vision for the Republic placed science, education and progress at the centre of a modern society. His era also encouraged a stronger national interest in aviation and the possibilities of the sky. It was a belief that knowledge, research and determined work could widen the horizon of what people might achieve.

TAVIRO takes inspiration from that spirit of curiosity: look beyond what is familiar, learn how things work and keep building toward the future.
