ABOUT · THE PROJECT

One core. Infinite void.

TAVIRO is an independent space-survival game built around a simple idea: keep moving, adapt to each run and discover how far one core can travel.

An independent game project from Türkiye

The developer

TAVIRO is created by Ahmet Eymen Bakraç, an independent Turkish developer. The project is developed independently, with a focus on learning, experimentation, accessible play and steady improvement.

Science, curiosity and the sky

Atatürk's vision for the Republic placed science, education and progress at the centre of a modern society. His era also encouraged a stronger national interest in aviation and the possibilities of the sky. This was not a claim about modern spaceflight; it was a belief that knowledge, research and determined work could widen the horizon of what people might achieve.

TAVIRO takes inspiration from that spirit of curiosity: look beyond what is familiar, learn how things work and keep building toward the future.

What guides TAVIRO

  • Independent development and honest communication.
  • A lightweight, offline-capable experience.
  • Fair play: no score is called verified without trustworthy server-side validation.
  • A game that grows carefully without losing its core identity.
